Our Projects

Genre: Action

Platforms: PC, consoles and digital stores

BLIND SPOT: Blind Spot is a fast-paced horror hybrid-casual action game where every second in the darkness could be your last. Step into a world overrun by terrifying zombie-like creatures and choose from three unique mercenaries Neo, Electra, and Luiz Armstrong each equipped to fight the horrors lurking in the shadows.
Battle through three eerie stages, each featuring increasingly dangerous waves of enemies, unique environments, and terrifying boss encounters. Clear every wave to progress, but don't get comfortable the longer you survive, the stronger, and deadlier the creatures become. Your light is your greatest weapon... and your greatest weakness. Stay alive, conserve your energy, and eliminate every monster before the darkness overwhelms you. Survive the horror. Conquer the waves. Embrace the darkness. How long can you last. Before the darkness claims you? Every run is a chance to beat your high score, master your favorite character, and discover the coolest ways to wipe out the undead.

Genre: A cultural Action-adventure, RPG

Setting: Inspired by Nigerian traditions and African storytelling

OTITE REBORN: Otite, a fearless and battle-hardened African warrior from the ancient Edo Kingdom, embarks on a perilous quest to vanquish a generational curse that has ravaged his people for generations. Fueled by determination and ancestral spirit, he ventures into the treacherous Evil Forest, confronting formidable demons and gods. As he battles to liberate his kingdom, Otite must master ancient abilities and harness the power of his heritage.
