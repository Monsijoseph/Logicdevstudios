About LogicDev

LogicDev is a game development studio founded in 2019 by brothers Chibuzor Armstrong Ifediogor and Mark C. Ifediogor, driven by a shared passion for expressive worlds and memorable play.

Based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, our growing team brings together art, engineering, production and storytelling to create games with a distinct African point of view.

Chibuzor Armstrong Ifediogor

Position: Founder and CEO, Lead Game Developer

Chibuzor Armstrong Ifediogor is the Founder of Logic Dev Studios, based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria.

With 7 years of experience in game development since 2019, he has collaborated with other studios on awesome titles. With Logic Dev Studios, he has worked on several very good looking games including Otite Reborn, Blind Spot, and Escape Protocol. Thug Love is currently in development.

He is the solo developer behind Blind Spot and Escape Protocol. On Otite Reborn, he serves as Lead Game Designer and Level Designer, and manages a team of 7 developers. Chibuzor specializes in Unity Engine with C# and Playmaker, Unreal Engine for world building and level design, and Blender for structural modular design for levels and props. He is also a game designer.

Mark C. Ifediogor

Position: Co-founder and Lead 2D and 3D artist

I’m a 2D/3D Artist, Animator, and Game Developer passionate about bringing compelling visual stories and interactive experiences to life.

As the Co-Founder of LogicDev, an independent Nigerian Game Studio, I work at the intersection of imagination and technical execution building worlds from initial concept art to full 3D animation and gameplay. When I’m not in the digital studio crafting games, you can find live on Twitch, streaming artwork sessions, game dev breakdowns, and hanging out with creative community.

Precious Otite Abraham

Position: CTO/Lead Programmer and VFX Artist

Precious Otite Abraham Lead Unreal Engine Developer, and Gameplay Programmer at Logicdev Studios, with over seven years of experience in game development.
He is responsible for the studio’s Unreal Engine development, including gameplay programming, combat systems, artificial intelligence, animation systems, player mechanics, technical implementation, optimization, and overall gameplay functionality. His technical expertise also extends to Roblox development and the creation of real-time visual effects in both Unreal Engine and Unity.
He has also delivered Unreal Engine and Roblox development solutions for clients across a range of gameplay systems and interactive projects.

He serves as the Lead Developer on OTITE: REBORN and Thug Love, translating creative and game-design visions into functional, responsive, and engaging gameplay experiences.
He is currently leading the development of Thug Love’s free-flow combat system, with a focus on fluid movement, intelligent enemy interactions, and dynamic player-driven combat.
